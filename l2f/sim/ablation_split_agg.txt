# group gradients conflict in most actor updates (last 0.6M steps: precision-efficiency 82% of

# split critics (--total 1) matched variant E on the circle (4.8 vs 5.0 cm) but crashed more (3.3 vs
# 0.8%). Can an aggregator turn the conflict into a better policy than the sum?
