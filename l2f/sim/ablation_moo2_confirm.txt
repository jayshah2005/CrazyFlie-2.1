# MOO round 2 on the time 0.5 recipe (ablation_aggregators: no MOO arm beat TD3; DualProj the only one
# at or above sum). Three questions:
#  1. Is DualProj's lead over sum real? 8 seeds each (70-73 reused from ablation_aggregators).
#  2. Why does even MOO sum train worse than TD3? Its actor step equals TD3's in expectation; the
#     critic loss differs: (sum_k e_k)^2 + total * sum_k e_k^2 with --total 1, against (sum e)^2 for
#     TD3. --total 0 keeps only the total-error loss (left: 4 output heads instead of 1); 0.1 between.
#  3. Does the aggregator help late? It acts mostly during the curriculum ramp (DualProj changed 83% of
#     the updates before 0.8M, 17% after 1.4M; UPGrad boosted safety and efficiency throughout).
#     --agg-start 1200000: the plain sum until the ramp (0.8M) and the weight caps (~1.2M) are done.
# Run: python l2f/sim/ablation.py --study l2f/sim/ablation_moo2.txt \
#        --build l2f/learning_to_fly/build_py_E_nb200/src --seeds 70 71 72 73 74 75 76 77 --jobs 12
# (td3 = ablation_time's huber_wp05_time05, seeds 70-77; moo4sum and moo4dualproj seeds 70-73 =
# ablation_aggregators'). The new arms below run seeds 70-73 only; see the run notes in reward_studies.md.
