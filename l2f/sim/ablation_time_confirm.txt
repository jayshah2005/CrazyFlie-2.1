# Ablation D2: the time cost on waypoint training. Base: crash 400, Huber 0.2, waypoints
# 0.3-0.5 m (20% of episodes, second leg 0.5-1x the jump) = ablation_waypoint's huber_wp05.
# Gate (reward_check.py, 8 policies): without Huber the waypoint reward fails even with no time
# cost (free flight 5-11%, waypoints up to 19%); with Huber, L2F_W_TIME 0.5 adds at most about one
# point (free flight 2.2-5.0%, waypoints 0.4-4.6%); 2 fails. Time cost 0.25 per step (0.5 x scale
# 0.5) is small far from the target but about the position cost 10 cm out, and "arrived" means
# within 5 cm and below 0.2 m/s: it mainly pays for closing the last 10 cm (the trained policies
# settle 9-11 cm off after a jump).
# Run: python l2f/sim/ablation.py --study l2f/sim/ablation_time.txt \
#        --build l2f/learning_to_fly/build_py_E_nb200/src --seeds 70 71 72 73 --jobs 12
# (crash400 and huber_wp05 are bit-identical to ablation_waypoint's arms, seeds 70-73)
