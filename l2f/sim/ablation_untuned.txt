# Untuned comparison (moo_findings.md, section 13): hand-tuned reward weights settle the trade-off
# before MOO starts, so they are an oracle, not the baseline. Here no group gets a hand-picked weight:
# --group-scale gives each of the 4 groups (time, precision, efficiency, safety without the crash
# term) the same mean cost per step, with the same total as the tuned reward (factors: medians over 4
# trained TD3 policies of the tuned time 0.5 recipe, reward_check.py "equal-share group scale"; the
# relative weights inside each group are kept). The crash penalty is fixed for every arm as the task's
# definition of failure: 600, the smallest that passed the reward check under this scale (400: free
# flight 7-8% FAIL for 3 of 4 policies; 600: at most 0.8% in any episode type).
# Task: the time 0.5 recipe (mission + 0.3-0.5 m waypoints at 20%, Huber 0.2, time cost, sim options).
# Baseline: TD3 on the untuned reward. MOO arms: head-scaled critic (--head-scale), --total 1; only the
# aggregator changes. No tuned TD3 in this study.
# Run: python l2f/sim/ablation.py --study l2f/sim/ablation_untuned.txt \
#        --build l2f/learning_to_fly/build_py_E_nb200/src --seeds 70 71 72 73 --jobs 12
